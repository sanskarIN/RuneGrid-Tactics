# Code of Conduct

RuneGrid Tactics welcomes contributors who communicate with care, curiosity, and respect. We expect discussion to focus on the work, welcome good-faith technical disagreement, and protect contributors from harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or exclusionary behavior.

Do not use sexualized language, publish private information, impersonate others, threaten participants, or make personal attacks. Maintain the game’s age-appropriate original identity and avoid content that creates a hostile community environment.

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and discussion that do not follow these standards. Concerns may be reported privately to **supportramsandesh@gmail.com**. Reports will be considered with discretion and without retaliation.
